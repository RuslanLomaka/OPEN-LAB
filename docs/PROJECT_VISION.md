# OPEN//LAB — Vision

**Tagline:** Stay curious. Pay less.

A curated discovery platform for people who want to try powerful AI tools without spending heavily on subscriptions. It covers AI coding, video, voice, 3D, automation, APIs and cloud services. Rather than being just another catalogue, it connects each offer with a concrete 10-minute learning experiment and practical multi-tool workflows.

## Target visitor
Curious developers, creators, students and experimenters who want to test capabilities before paying.

## Distinctive features
- Transparent distinction between free tiers, one-time credits, trials and paid promotions.
- Official source, date checked, region, expiry, billing requirements and license limitations.
- Small practical challenges and workflows connecting multiple tools.
- Save favorites locally; eventually support accounts, notifications and contributed corrections.

## Editorial principle
Never pass an unverified deal off as valid. The current site is a static demonstration with sample listings.
